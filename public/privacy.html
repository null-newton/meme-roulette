Privacy & Safety

Last updated 8 November 2026

Meme Roulette is a party game where players caption photos from each other's phones. Because the game shows personal photos to other people, every photo is opt-in, can be removed at any time, and is deleted when the game ends.

Photos

  • Purpose: photos are used only to show them to the other players in your room.
  • Opt-in: only photos you confirm on the preview screen leave your phone.
  • Prepared on your phone: before anything is shared, each photo is resized and re-encoded on your device, which removes all metadata, including GPS location.
  • Never on a server: photos travel directly between players' phones over encrypted WebRTC connections. No server stores them, and none relays them.
  • Retention: other phones keep your photos only in memory for that room. They are deleted when you leave, when the game ends, or when you remove a photo. Your "Saved stash" for the next game is stored only on your own device.
  • Saving memes: another player can only save a meme made from your photo if you approve it at that moment. No answer within 10 seconds counts as no.

Nudity is never allowed

  • An image classifier runs on your phone on the preview screen. Photos it flags as likely nudity are blocked and can't be shared, whatever the game settings.
  • The classifier runs entirely on your device; your photos are never sent anywhere to be checked.
  • Automated checks aren't perfect, so reporting is the second line of defence (below).

Reporting, hiding and removing

  • Long-press any photo to Report it, Hide it for yourself, or Remove your own photo from the game.
  • Three reports, or the host, remove a photo from the game for everyone.
  • Write-in captions are limited to 80 characters and filtered for slurs and profanity.
  • The host can remove players; their photos leave the game immediately.

Other data

  • No accounts: you join with a nickname and avatar, kept in your browser's local storage.
  • Matchmaking: to connect phones, a matchmaking service passes connection details (including network addresses) between players in the same room. It never receives game content or photos.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking.

Age

Meme Roulette is for players 17 and older.

Contact

Questions, abuse reports or data requests: open an issue at github.com/null-newton/meme-roulette/issues.
